# Invented multi observation tutorial
All values are invented teaching records, not solver results or measurements.
A and B are hypothetical response variants with a common initial temperature of 300 K and the same stipulated forcing and boundary conditions.
The profile uses instantaneous temperature samples over the exact saved 0 to 5 s window; the 9 s records are excluded. The mean uses nonuniform trapezoidal spacing.
Timing records are independently supplied event times, not thresholds interpolated from the temperature profile. The reported timing range is an invented illustrative range, not a confidence interval.
Temperature comparison is at exactly 5 s in both variants. The 430 K center and 10 K half width are declared illustrative bounds, not independent experimental data.
Signed difference is model minus reference center. Outside distance is signed relative to the nearest bound and zero only for an available value within its supplied interval. Missing bounds remain unavailable.
No geometry, transport field, reaction law or literature is supplied. The observations distinguish timing from temperature agreement but cannot identify a physical cause.
