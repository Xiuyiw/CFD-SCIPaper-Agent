# Synthetic study definition
Lower and upper are non-overlapping and exhaust the wetted wall in each case.
area [m2] is region area; rate [W] is integrated heat entering the coolant on that region.
Reference and Modified have the same inlet and wall boundary conditions; geometry differs.
The data are invented solely to demonstrate the software. They are not measured or simulated results.
Total heat rate and area-average heat flux are different quantities; the decomposition alone does not prove a flow mechanism.
